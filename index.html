Blog: Holy Shift

There are plenty of reasons people fall in love with coding. For me, it’s not just about technology, problem-solving, or building cool projects — it’s about freedom and the life I want to live.

In my past work, I was constantly on the move. Flying to different places, chasing opportunities, meeting new faces — on the outside it looked exciting. But the reality was, I was missing out on things that really mattered.

I missed simple evenings with my girlfriend. I missed family meals with my grandparents. I missed playing with my dogs, or even just sitting at home and being present. All the traveling and hustle made me realize something important: I don’t just want a career, I want a life that lets me be close to the people I love.

That’s where coding comes in. For me, learning web development is more than just a career switch — it’s a way to build a future where I don’t have to trade time with family for work. Coding gives me the chance to work from home, to create projects that excite me, and to build something meaningful while still being there for the people (and dogs!) I care about.

Every time I write a new function, solve a bug, or push a project live, it feels like I’m one step closer to that balance. That’s why I’m passionate about coding: because it’s not just lines of code on a screen, it’s the bridge between my skills, my goals, and the kind of life I want to live.
